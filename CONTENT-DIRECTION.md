# NovaCrest Technologies — Content & Brand Voice Direction
**Governing Specification:** `NovaCrest_Antigravity_Master_MultiAgent_Prompt.txt`

---

## 1. Brand Voice Principles
- **Confident, Not Arrogant**: We speak with authority based on engineering craft, not corporate posturing.
- **Human & Conversational**: Direct sentences, active voice, real problem-solving language.
- **Specific & Outcome-Driven**: Explain what the software actually achieves (speed, customer conversion, workflow automation) rather than listing abstract features.
- **Zero Corporate Jargon**: Banished words include *“cutting-edge”, “innovative solutions”, “seamless synergy”, “disruptive”, “end-to-end transformation”*.

1. **Hero**: Ambitious businesses need software built with precision, not recycled templates.
2. **Services**: What we build (Web, Mobile, Custom Software, AI) demonstrated through tangible deliverables.
